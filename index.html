New here? Try it in 30 seconds

HEALA tells a hospital that a patient is coming before she leaves, and gets a yes. Our nurse teammate says that today, a hospital that cannot treat a patient gives her a paper referral letter and does not warn the next hospital.

  1. Press All screens, then Run guided demo. In about 20 seconds you will see the midwife's phone, the hospital phones and the state dashboard.
  2. Then try it yourself: choose Nurse (referring), press Dial referral line, and answer the menu.
  3. Choose Hospital and reply for the hospital. Then open State dashboard to see times and decline reasons.
